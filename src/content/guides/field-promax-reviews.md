### Counting seats in a real shop

The captured text does not say who counts as a user. Here is an illustrative case. An HVAC shop has a dispatcher, an office manager and four technicians. That is six people, which lands in Premium's described range if every person needs a login. If the technicians only need job details on a phone, the count could differ. The pages do not answer that, so ask.

A one-truck owner-operator sits in the described Light range. The same owner hiring a second tech and a part-time scheduler crosses into Standard's range. Plan for that jump before you build habits around one tier's feature list.

## Questions to ask before a demo

**Ask the vendor for written answers on seat definitions, current pricing, sync direction and edition coverage before any demo. Six questions cover the gaps this page cannot close. Use them with your own dispatcher, technicians and bookkeeper in the room, because they feel the gaps first.**

1. Which roles count as a user: office staff, technicians, a part-time scheduler, a bookkeeper?
2. What is the current rate for the tier you need, is it monthly or annual, and what is the add-on past 8 users?
3. Does two-way sync cover your exact QuickBooks Online, QuickBooks Desktop or Xero setup?
4. Which records sync: customers, invoices, payments, estimates, or work orders themselves?
5. What happens to a callback or warranty visit that should not bill?
6. Which tier holds the features you need today, and can you see that in writing?

Bring one real week of jobs to the demo, including a rescheduled call and a job with a parts return. Ask the vendor to walk those through, not a sample job.
