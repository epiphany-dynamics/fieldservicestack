## Checklist to verify before you rely on any claim

**Before relying on any advertised capability, confirm it in your own account, on your own plan, with a real booking. This checklist turns each vendor sentence into a test you can run, so a crew's Monday schedule never depends on a marketing page.**

1. **Booking form.** Embed it on a test page. Submit a booking from a phone. Confirm every custom field appears on the resulting job.
2. **Recurring series.** Create a biweekly booking with a frequency discount. Reschedule one visit and cancel another. Note what the series does.
3. **Reminders.** Book a test client with your own email and phone. Record when the reminder arrives and what it says.
4. **Payments.** Pre-authorize a test card, complete the job, then charge. Try a decline and note what staff see.
5. **Integrations.** Ask support in writing which plan includes Stripe, QuickBooks, Google Calendar and Twilio.
6. **CSV export.** Download a date range with every available field. List what is missing.
7. **Date the evidence.** Save screenshots with today's date.
