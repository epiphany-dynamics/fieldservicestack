**Move from FieldVibe Solo Free to Solo Pro when manual reminders, hand-written quotes or invoices, and payment chasing cost you real hours. Move to Crews when a second person needs access, roles, or job assignments. Prices and limits below come from the vendor's pricing page, not from a hands-on test.**

This page is not a star rating. It uses FieldVibe's own published plan descriptions to show where each purchase boundary sits and what daily signals suggest you have crossed it. Check current regional pricing before you buy.

## What each FieldVibe tier includes

**FieldVibe lists three plans: Solo (free) for one person with manual reminders, Solo Pro at $20 a month adding automation, quotes, invoices and payments, and Crews at $50 a month adding unlimited staff and permissions. The vendor states all prices in USD and says final price varies by region.**

| Capability (vendor-listed) | Solo (free) | Solo Pro ($20/mo) | Crews ($50/mo) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Users | 1 user, single device | 1 user, multi-device | Unlimited staff |
| Jobs and clients | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Reminders | Semi-automated, from your own phone | Automated, dedicated number | Automated, higher quota |
| Quotes, invoices, online payments | Not listed | Listed | Listed |
| Time and revenue tracking, tasks | Not listed | Listed | Listed |
| Roles and permissions | Not listed | Not listed | Listed |
| Multi-staff job assignment | Not listed | Not listed | Listed |

Listed automated reminder quotas are 400 a month in the US and Canada (150 globally) on Solo Pro, and 1,000 (400 globally) on Crews. Free also includes a business website for collecting work requests.

## Signs you have outgrown Solo Free

**Outgrowing Solo Free usually shows up as repeated admin: sending reminders by hand, rebuilding quotes outside the app, and chasing unpaid invoices. If those tasks eat time you would otherwise bill, Solo Pro is the next step. Free stays fine while the volume is low.**

Free reminders are semi-automated. The app prepares the message, but it goes out from your own phone, so you still have to be the one who sends it. Fully automated reminders from a dedicated number are a paid feature, and nothing here should be read as free doing that job.

Here is an illustrative case. A solo pressure washer books eight driveways a week and texts each customer the night before. Two customers no-show in a month, and each costs a lost slot. The reminder work is small, but the invoicing is not: she writes quotes in a notes app and invoices on Sunday night. That Sunday hour is the signal, not the reminder volume.

### Signals that point to Solo Pro

- You send quotes outside the app and retype them into invoices.
- Customers pay late because there is no online payment link.
- You switch between a phone and a laptop and need the same data on both.
- You want hours and revenue by job without a spreadsheet.

If your question is only free scheduling, the free tier covers jobs and clients already. Our roundup on [software carpet cleaning companies use](/guides/what-software-do-carpet-cleaning-companies-use/) looks at how free solo scheduling fits that trade, so this page does not repeat it.

## Signs you need Crews instead of Solo Pro

**Solo Pro is a one-user plan. The moment someone else must log in, receive assigned jobs, or be kept out of billing, you are past it. Crews at $50 a month adds unlimited staff, custom roles and permissions, and multi-staff job assignment.**

Picture a two-person drain cleaning outfit. The owner runs estimates and invoices. A helper drives to the morning calls. If the helper needs to see the day's callbacks but not prices or customer payment history, that is a permissions question, and permissions are listed only on Crews.

Another case: a lawn crew of three splits one large property across two trucks. Multi-staff job assignment matters there, because a single owner login cannot assign one job to two people. ### What not to infer

Crews adds people and control. The vendor does not say it adds dispatch maps, route optimization or accounting sync, so do not assume it. ## A four-question upgrade check

**Answer four questions in order: do you work alone, do you invoice by hand, does anyone else need access, and does anyone need limited access. The first yes after "work alone" marks the tier. Stop at the cheapest plan that removes your current bottleneck.**

1. **Are you the only person using the account?** If not, you need Crews. Stop here.
2. **Do you quote or invoice outside the app?** If yes, price Solo Pro.
3. **Do you need online payments or time tracking?** If yes, Solo Pro again.
4. **Does a helper need job access but not billing?** If yes, Crews.

Then run a simple cost test with your own numbers. Illustrative only: if Sunday invoicing costs you 1 hour and you value an hour at $40, the $20 Solo Pro fee is cheaper than the hour you spend each week. Replace those figures with yours, and confirm what your region's bill and message quota actually are.

## Limits of this review

**This is a read of vendor documentation, not a test. It gives no star rating, no measured time savings and no claim that migration or integrations work. Verify current pricing, regional reminder quotas and your own data export needs directly with FieldVibe before paying.**

Three cautions apply. First, "Not listed" in the table means the pricing page did not list it, not that it is absent. Second, regional billing and included sends vary, so a US-based quota may not match yours. Third, if you already run several people and callbacks daily, jumping to a larger platform may suit you better than climbing tiers one at a time. Do not move up a tier just because the next one exists.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is FieldVibe free for a one-person business?

The vendor lists a free Solo plan with unlimited jobs and clients, manual reminders, single-device access and a business website for work requests. It does not list quotes, invoices or online payments on that tier.

### Are free FieldVibe reminders automatic?

Not fully. The vendor describes free reminders as semi-automated messages sent from your own phone. Fully automated reminders from a dedicated number are listed on paid plans.

### Do I need Crews if I only have one helper?

If the helper needs their own login, assigned jobs or restricted access, yes. Solo Pro is a one-user plan, and roles and permissions are listed only on Crews.

### Does Solo Pro replace accounting software?

The vendor lists invoices, online payments and revenue tracking, but this review did not test them. Whether it fits your books depends on your accountant and process, so check before you drop other tools.
